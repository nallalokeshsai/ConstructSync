## Strategic Advantages of ConstructSync
1. **Prescription, Not Just Analytics**: Automatically blocks payment when damaged goods are billed and provides the exact credit note deduction figure.
2. **Construction Yard Ready**: Designed for site engineers with poor network coverage, allowing indent creation with instant offline synchronization.
3. **Zero Financial Drift**: Enforces multi-level approval thresholds so site teams cannot order above ₹1,00,000 without Director sign-off.
4. **Indian Tax & Audit Localization**: Tailored for Indian compliance with GST verification, e-way bill check compatibility, and statutory 194C/194Q TDS deductions.
